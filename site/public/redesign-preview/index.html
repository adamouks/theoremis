01 / HOW IT WORKS

From “what if?” to
“I’ve got it.”

Research isn't a straight line. Theoremis gives you one place to think, try things, make mistakes, and make progress.

↥

Start wherever you are

Open a blank page or bring in a paper, handwritten notes, LaTeX, or an existing document. Pick up where you left off.

✳

Ask AI to explain a step, spot a gap, suggest a different approach, or help you draw a graph. You choose what to keep.

✓

Know what's checked

Keep suggestions separate from facts. For formal proofs, use Lean to check whether each step is valid.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

AI can suggest an answer.
It can't declare it true.

Theoremis makes the difference visible. AI helps you reason; proof checking gives you stronger confidence. You're always the one deciding what belongs in your work.

03 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions,
answered plainly.

No catches, no mysterious claims. Here's what matters before you try it.

Is Theoremis free?

Yes. It's free and open source, and it's currently in public beta.

Does AI prove everything for me?

No. AI can explore ideas, draft and review reasoning, and attempt formal proofs. Its suggestions are not automatically correct. Lean can formally check a proof, and you can see what has or hasn't been verified.

Can I use my own AI?

Yes. Theoremis supports ChatGPT and local models through Ollama. These integrations are in beta.

Does it run on my computer?

Yes. Theoremis is a local-first desktop app for macOS, Windows, and Linux. Your documents live in files you control.
